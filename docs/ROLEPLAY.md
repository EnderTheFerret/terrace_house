# Dialogue models and the Terrace House feel

For the October 6 active configuration, matched prompt/temperature tests and remaining failures, see [DIALOGUE-INVESTIGATION.md](DIALOGUE-INVESTIGATION.md). Dialogue now selects Rocinante X 12B locally; the Gemma-only selection described below is historical. The [lorebook guide](LOREBOOK.md) describes the source-backed World Info book and its game integration.

The core is six people with independent daily lives, relationships and ambitions. Netflix describes the format as unscripted shared-house life, and its episode descriptions include work, cooking, dating and difficult conversations. Sources: [Netflix production announcement](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/netflix-starts-production-on-new-series-of-terrace-house-in-karuizawa-japan), [Boys & Girls in the City](https://www.netflix.com/gb/title/80067942), [academic analysis of daily life, work and voluntary departures](https://mediarep.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/ebe401f6-04ed-4f09-bf94-10e8b6328115/content).

Design inference: the valuable missing layer was follow-through. An invitation creates something to anticipate; showing up or missing it changes trust; career progress creates a reason to stay or graduate. Let ordinary chores, quiet talks, friendships and refusals matter. The player should discover routines, choose whom to spend limited time with, and see how the house responds. No score for dating everyone or forcing drama. A shared calendar, visible mess, small favors, career arcs, notification/feed callbacks and a player-chosen finale make these existing systems easier to enjoy.

## October 4 conversation investigation

The screenshot's "About already... yeah" is deterministic fallback text, not necessarily Gemma's reply. The fallback selected the longest non-stopword in the player's message and appended it to a generic intent acknowledgment. This word echo is removed. The voice validator also rejected any mention of "the show" or "the camera", even ordinary dialogue answering the player's filming joke. It now rejects camera narration and audience/panel commentary while allowing relevant filming dialogue. AI, prompt and character meta checks remain.

The line prompt previously forced every first response to acknowledge a request or boundary, and only the first group responder received the latest typed topic. All responders now follow the current exchange; ordinary offers get a practical answer and only actual refusals need a boundary acknowledgment. Persona cards present mannerisms as occasional tendencies and omit forced tells. Required context is shortened before final output instructions can be cut off. Conversation time is doubled to six minutes per spoken line and updates at each turn, with legacy time logs retaining three minutes per line.

`scripts/conversation-probe.ts` runs three fixed two-turn fixtures: Kai's filming/coffee exchange, Ren's breakfast disagreement and a two-housemate coffee offer. Baseline: `logs/conversation-probe-before-1791116052695.json`. Final matched comparison: `logs/conversation-probe-verified-1791116676504.json`. Gemma returned all eight required NPC lines; group replies answered the coffee offer and carrying question. Kai still uses exaggerated slang ("vibes", "move", "bro"); this prompt pass does not establish fully natural dialogue. The installed Stheno RP model was tested at temperature 1.15 and its production sampler settings: it invented player speech in two turns, omitted Ren's required answer in one, and added actions and unsupported details. Keep Gemma as the current model. These six turns per model are a small semantic probe, not a comprehensive benchmark or a stable speed comparison.

For a new specialist to evaluate, [Rocinante X 12B v1](https://huggingface.co/TheDrummer/Rocinante-X-12B-v1) is the successor to Rocinante v1.1; its author explicitly targets roleplay and dialogue. Its card specifies Mistral v3 Tekken (without `[SYSTEM_PROMPT]`) or Metharme. This is a candidate based on the author's description, not a measured improvement here; it was not installed or selected. [Stheno's original card](https://huggingface.co/Sao10K/L3-8B-Stheno-v3.2) also explicitly covers roleplaying and supplies sampler recommendations, so the comparison does include a specialist rather than another generic chat model.

[Chun121 Qwen3-4B RPG Roleplay V2](https://huggingface.co/Chun121/Qwen3-4B-RPG-Roleplay-V2) is a smaller specialist: its [original card](https://huggingface.co/Chun121/Qwen3-4B-RPG-Roleplay-V2/raw/main/README.md) lists Q4_K_M around 2.5 GB, a 2048-token context and a custom `<thinking>` / `<RESPONSE>` format. It rewards longer narration and actions, whereas this game expects short `speaker_id: text` lines. It therefore needs a prompt/output adapter before a fair comparison; it is not the first replacement to try. The card labels the license MIT but contains an Apache label inside model-index metadata, so treat the terms as inconsistent. Its mature training data also needs the game's existing content checks.

[Gemma 3 4B](https://ollama.com/library/gemma3:4b) is an easy fallback at 3.3 GB Q4_K_M. [Gemma 4 E2B/E4B](https://ollama.com/library/gemma4) are available, but effective parameter counts hide embeddings: E2B has 5.1B total parameters and E4B has 8B total; the listed downloads start at 4.6 GB and 6.6 GB. E4B is a modest weight reduction from the existing 12B model. Neither was measured here. Keep the current Gemma default until the smaller candidate passes the game's dialogue comparison.

### Memory and continuity

Each character already saves personal memories; compaction keeps the 40 highest importance-times-recency entries. Dialogue includes up to three relevant memories, current relationship summaries, known facts and recent lines under its prompt budget. Pair summaries and memories are part of the SQLite save snapshot, so loading a save restores them. The character bible now exposes your own shared history with each housemate, rather than their private thoughts. Summaries are deterministic and bounded, not an extra LLM call. Low-priority memories may fall out of the prompt or be discarded at compaction; this supports continuity but cannot promise perfect lifelong recall.

## Integration and evaluation

`OLLAMA_MODEL` remains the structured JSON model. `OLLAMA_MODEL_LINES` defaults to that same model and changes only scene dialogue, typed replies and direct chat. Persona creation uses structured JSON, validates each field independently, keeps identity/traits/job schedule, retries similar exemplar voices once, and logs snapshots for replay. Both adapters have independent health checks and template fallbacks.

When two different models are selected, Ollama unloads after each request by default so both models do not remain resident beside ComfyUI. `OLLAMA_KEEP_ALIVE=30m` restores residency when your GPU can accommodate it; `0` releases after each request. Reloading trades latency for memory.

Run `npx tsx scripts/roleplay-benchmark.ts gemma4:12b candidate-name` after installing candidates. Every model sees the same seeded 20 scenes. Reports under `logs/` include raw output, mechanical voice/content pass rate, missing lines, unexpected/player speakers, voice-distance matrix, generation tokens/second, elapsed time and GPU memory when `nvidia-smi` exists. No repair or template fallback masks raw failures. Read outputs to evaluate consent, knowledge leakage, varied voices and whether conversations are pleasant. A regex cannot prove PG-13 compliance.

For coexistence testing, generate a ComfyUI image immediately before the benchmark and keep its process running. Record load/offload logs and device-wide VRAM; availability alone does not prove named model residency. The benchmark defaults to `keep_alive: 0`; use `--keep-alive=30m` to retain the tested model during its scenes. Wall time includes loading while tokens/second uses Ollama evaluation duration.

The October 2 preparation imported the verified 4,920,734,432-byte Stheno Q4_K_M file, SHA-256 `c3a4bc66c1e7b1b196c4c5121538b512cffd5039d40bb5ee1bb9fee3c917e780`, through Ollama's Hugging Face support. `ollama show` exposed an automatically selected ChatML template, contrary to the original author's Llama-3-Instruct instructions. The local evaluation model `terrace-stheno:8b-q4` therefore uses [the checked-in Modelfile](models/SthenoQ4.Modelfile) with the author's template, stops and sampler defaults. It reuses the downloaded weight layer. Gemma remains the game default. No inference ran during import.

The benchmark now captures Ollama `/api/ps`, ComfyUI `/system_stats` and its existing `/internal/logs` before and after scenes, and saves partial failures. `--keep-alive=30m` tests continued LLM residency within each model's 20 scenes; the default remains `0`. It explicitly unloads each speaking model before testing the next, and confirms the model disappeared from `/api/ps`. New runs fix the sampling seed to `20 + scene index` and use the same game dialogue temperature and 8192 context for each candidate; the earlier baseline had no explicit sampling seed. Model-specific sampler defaults remain in effect, so results compare the configured candidates, not quantization alone. Named ComfyUI load/cache logs give stronger evidence than availability, but async weight offloading means cached image models do not necessarily remain entirely in GPU memory. Report device allocation and log evidence separately from full residency.

## Human review of the October 1 raw Gemma baseline

The mechanical pass rate overstates conversation quality. All five injected refusal prompts (arrival-intro, shared-car-date, silent-breakfast, cafe-date and morning-run) lack a first-line acknowledgment. In cafe-date, Shun instead proposes a bistro and later expresses romantic enjoyment of the company. The output is not explicit or overtly coercive, but it does not establish reliable refusal handling. These old fixtures gave the refusal to a player absent from the scene's NPC-only role binding, which weakens their realism.

No `player:` labels appear, but every scene binds NPCs only, so that result is a weak test of preserving player agency. The shared-car-date invents Ren's parents restricting his cooking and his childhood soup-making; those details are not present in the supplied persona/knowledge sections. That is unsupported biography, rather than a demonstrated leak of another character's secret. No planted unknown-secret probe was included; this baseline cannot prove knowledge boundaries.

The October 2 comparison corrects all five refusal fixtures: the player is now the counterpart, and the model may produce only the NPC's beats. Each model receives identical cloned scenario states, templates and sampling seeds; reports retain full prompts, role bindings and expected speakers for inspection. These corrected fixtures and fixed sampling seeds make the new run noncomparable with the old baseline's aggregate pass rate. The remaining same-morning weather/fallback-topic limitations still apply.

After the dialogue comparison, `npx tsx scripts/generation-probe.ts --run-real` checks one real structured persona and appearance request in an isolated state, including pinned identity/traits/schedule and exact generated-snapshot replay. It opens no game save or database; raw responses and checks go under `logs/`. Run it only after ComfyUI's artwork batch releases the GPU.

The voices differ at the surface: Ren uses short fragments, Shun formal phrases, Kaito repeated "bro"/"vibe", and Mio hesitant "um". Their conversation content is repetitive: 12 of 20 scenes explicitly discuss rain, birthday never mentions a birthday or cake, and karaoke-night never mentions singing. The same rainy morning state and fallback beat topics partly cause this repetition. Backyard-talk includes Sora saying she feels she is performing under many eyes, despite the prompt's rule against camera/audience commentary. Unlabeled silence rows in backyard-talk and shared-car-date also break the requested speaker format. These examples justify keeping the comparison exploratory and reviewing its actual prose before changing the game's default model.

At the initial October 1 probe Ollama was reachable with `gemma4:12b`, `llava` and `llama3.2` installed; the three RP candidates were absent and ComfyUI at port 8188 was unavailable.

The Gemma 4 12B baseline completed all 20 scenes on October 1, 2026: 132 expected lines, 96.97% mechanical voice/content pass, four missing lines, zero unexpected/player speaker labels, zero explicit-content regex hits, 24.96 generation tokens/second and 207.16 seconds wall time. Device-wide peak GPU memory was 15,381 MiB; ComfyUI was unavailable. The lowest sampled pairwise voice distance was 0.3244. Raw outputs and the complete matrix are in `logs/roleplay-benchmark-1790877239662.json`. These numbers describe this run, and need human review; they do not prove safe semantics, realism, or RP-model superiority.
